02 · Proposed Solution
SIH 2026 · SIH26145

Six threats, six passive signals, one live verdict

The real setting
Power grids and defence links are watched through a data diode: traffic is copied one way so the monitor can never become a way in.
Why it's hard
No probing, no handshakes, no decryption, no blocking. Most tools assume two-way access. Passive-only, streaming, multi-threat detection is rare.
Our answer
A streaming pipeline where each threat gets its own detector, and every alert carries the evidence that fired it.
What it detects, from metadata alone
  • Floods, beacons, scans, tunnels, exfiltration — each read from a different statistical fingerprint, not a payload.
  • Alerts stream in with bounded latency and a standard schema: time, flow, threat class, confidence, evidence.
  • Runs entirely offline on a read-only feed. Nothing is ever sent back across the tap.
Innovation · what no one else builds

Malware caught inside encrypted traffic

Everyone inspects payloads. We can't, and don't need to. Using JA3 / JA4 TLS fingerprints plus packet-size and timing patterns, we flag malware inside encrypted TLS/QUIC sessions without decrypting a byte — the hardest ask in the brief, and the one most teams skip.

04 · Feasibility & Viability
SIH 2026 · SIH26145

The data is public, the models are light, the demo is honest

Why it is feasible
  • Labelled data already exists. CIC-IDS-2017/2018 and CTU-13 ship attack timelines, so every detector has ground truth to train and prove against.
  • No GPU needed. Per-threat models are lightweight (entropy stats, tree ensembles), fast enough to run streaming on a laptop.
  • Streaming is standard. Windowed processing with per-flow state, then replay a capture at a controlled rate to show it live.
Grand-finale MVP: a live dashboard replaying real traffic, 4 detectors including the encrypted-malware one, a stated throughput, and F1 measured against a simple baseline.
Risks, and how we beat them

"Is it really live?"

We replay a real capture at a set rate and show bounded per-alert latency and sustained flows/sec, not an end-of-run summary.

Encrypted-malware accuracy

We pair a known-bad JA3/JA4 list with a trained metadata classifier and report honest precision/recall. We claim detection support, never certainty.

False positives on a monitor that can't block

Alerts are ranked by confidence with evidence attached, so analysts triage the top of the list. We measure FP rate against a baseline.

  • Critical infrastructure is watched passively for a reason. This makes that safe, one-way vantage genuinely intelligent instead of just a recorder.
  • Encrypted traffic is now the norm. Detecting threats without decryption keeps monitoring lawful, private and evidentially clean.
  • Explainable alerts fight fatigue. Every alert names the feature that fired, so analysts act on signal instead of drowning in noise.
